Help
How to Update Results
  1. Select the game you want to update from the dropdown list.
  2. Choose the appropriate result based on the outcome of the game.
  3. Click "Update Results" to apply the result to all pending value bets for that game.
Result Types
  • Home Win: The home team won the game.
  • Away Win: The away team won the game.
  • Draw: The game ended in a tie/draw.
  • Over: The total points/goals exceeded the over/under line.
  • Under: The total points/goals were below the over/under line.
  • Push: The result exactly matched the spread or total, resulting in a tie.
  • Cancelled: The game was cancelled or postponed.
